# Human and physical checks before commercial sale

- Have an independent woodworker build a full-size prototype from this manual alone; record fit, missing instructions, drill access and actual screw paths.
- Verify actual timber species/grade/moisture, plywood thickness/voids/stiffness and adhesive procedure; test the specific dowel, cleat, lap and screw joints.
- Select actual top angles, bracing steel and restraint products; verify dimensions, screw tips, fastener installation guidance and wall compatibility.
- Have a qualified reviewer define and perform load, sustained-load creep, racking, impact, tipping and installed-restraint tests; establish evidence before publishing any working-load claim.
- Inspect edges, splinters, exposed steel, stability, finish cure, climbing/entrapment risks and intended-market product requirements.
- Proof the final cut list, drawings, assembly sequence, hardware availability and 1:1 pattern print scale against the completed prototype.
- Confirm rights for the original publication, brand and artwork; approve a new commercial revision only after corrections and revalidation.

None of these physical checks has been performed in this digital task. No safe working load or structural certification is provided.
